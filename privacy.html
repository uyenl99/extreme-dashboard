Privacy Policy

Effective August 23, 2026

Information collected

We may collect your email, account credentials, support communications, subscription status, and standard technical and security data. Stripe processes payment-card information; Extreme Trading Inc. does not store complete card numbers.

Information we do not request for the service

We do not request your brokerage credentials, portfolio holdings, finances, investment objectives, or risk tolerance. Do not send that information in support messages.

Use and service providers

Information is used to authenticate accounts, provide access, manage subscriptions, deliver service messages, answer permitted support questions, maintain security, prevent abuse, and comply with law. Providers may include Vercel, Google Analytics, Supabase, Stripe, email services, and Collective2, each operating under its own privacy practices.

Website analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and site usage. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies and process information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser information, referral source, and interaction events. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, payment information, or account credentials to Google Analytics. Google processes this information under its own privacy practices. You can limit analytics cookies through your browser settings.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers, as legally required, to protect rights and security, or in a business transaction. Records are retained as reasonably needed for service, security, tax, dispute, and legal purposes.

Your choices and contact

To request access, correction, or deletion, contact support@extremetradinginc.com. Some records may be retained where required.

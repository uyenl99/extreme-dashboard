About Extreme Trading Inc.

Model-generated market research, systematic signals, and historical strategy information.

Our Mission

Extreme Trading Inc. develops systematic trading strategies designed to identify opportunities through data analysis, statistical research, and disciplined execution.

Our member product is an impersonal information subscription. Members receive the same strategy information; we do not tailor signals to anyone's portfolio, finances, objectives, or risk tolerance.

Research Philosophy

Markets are uncertain and constantly evolving. We believe consistency comes from following well-defined rules, managing risk carefully, and continuously evaluating results.

Research Process

  1. Strategy idea generation
  2. Historical testing and validation
  3. Risk and drawdown analysis
  4. Live deployment and monitoring
  5. Ongoing performance review

Technology

Our infrastructure utilizes automation, cloud deployment, data analytics, and performance reporting tools to support strategy development and monitoring.

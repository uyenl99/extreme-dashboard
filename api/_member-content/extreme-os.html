Member FAQ

Member guide for reading alerts and results.

Where should I look first?

Check Today's Orders first, then Current Open Positions. Historical trades provide context but are not current instructions.

When is the page updated?

The site refreshes from the latest published Collective2 data. Always check the displayed update time before acting.

Why can my fill differ?

Broker timing, spreads, liquidity, order handling, account size, and missed alerts can make personal results differ from the model.

Are alerts personalized advice?

No. Alerts report model activity. Members remain responsible for execution, sizing, taxes, and risk decisions.
